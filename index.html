Our four verification principles

We publish exactly how we decide what's safe. "Muslim-friendly" and "halal-certified" are always clearly distinguished — no vague labels, ever.

01
Principle 01

No pork, no alcohol

Products containing pork or added alcohol are never listed.

02
Principle 02

No shared facilities

Products made in facilities that also process haram items are excluded.

03
Principle 03

No contamination risk

Any product with a cross-contamination possibility is excluded.

04
Principle 04

Certified meat only

Meat-based products are listed only after halal certification review.
